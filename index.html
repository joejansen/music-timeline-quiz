Welcome to

Trackback

Place each music moment on the timeline in the order it happened.

  1. Drag the clue to a gap in the timeline, or just tap the gap.
  2. Lock it in. The year is revealed and the card settles where it truly belongs.
  3. You have three lives. The third miss ends the run — your score is how many you got right.

Moments from the same year count on either side of each other. There’s a new mix every day, and your progress is saved on this device.
